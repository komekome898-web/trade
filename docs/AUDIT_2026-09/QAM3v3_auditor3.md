# QAM3v3 — blind audit (auditor 3)

Independent re-implementation of the maker fill model from the packet's own rule text; no repository simulator, generator or answer file was opened. Script: `scratchpad/audit_QAM3v3_3.py` (variant runners `_var/_grid/_val` derived from it).

**Data**: `backtest_data/qa_known_answer_maker3_v3_20260905/` — ticker 340,617 rows, executions 36,904 rows, 2026-08-03T00:00Z→08-07T24:00Z (5.00 days; no non-monotonic timestamps; 1,030 duplicate ticker timestamps; largest gap 30.5 s). Own clip 0.05, tick 10.0, mean spread 2.33 bps (2.33 ticks), price ~100k. **Fee used**: 0 bps maker and taker — manifest, confirmed by `config/products.yaml` `FX_BTC_JPY: taker_fee_pct: 0.0` (`swap_daily_pct: 0.06` = 6 bps/day → ≤0.01 bps over a ≤300 s hold). Every "net" below is therefore also gross; no cost quoted in a claim was trusted.

**Primary implementation (P)** = the rule as literally written: full-fill FIFO, queue-ahead = displayed size at insertion − 0.05, exact-price matching of opposite-taker-side prints, cancel/re-join on touch move, exit inserted at the post-trade touch, forced exit crossing the displayed touch at 300 s. Three phrases in the rule are ambiguous, so I ran all 16 combinations of: partial fill opens a position or not; queue-ahead with/without the own-size subtraction; exact-price vs price-through matching (a resting bid filled by taker sells at ≤ its price). **Through-matching (T)** is reported for S2 because under exact-price matching an *improved* quote can only fill if a public print lands on a price nobody was quoting, making the more aggressive strategy fill 70% *less* than S1 — physically backwards, and contradicted by the manifest's own "improving ⇒ queue-ahead = 0 by construction".

Controls (Q2), S1 population, rule P: sign-reversed = −0.066 bps (0-fee ⇒ exactly symmetric); shuffled taker-side labels = −0.148 bps, t=−1.10, n=877; **random-entry placebo** (open at the touch at 1,436 uniformly random times, then the *same* maker exit + 300 s cap) = **+0.978 bps, t=10.72, forced 44.6%** — entering at random times earns ~15× the strategy's own edge and reproduces the claimed forced share exactly. Money (Q3): 0.05 × ~100,600 = 5,030 JPY notional/round trip; +0.066 bps = 0.033 JPY/RT × 287 RT/day = **9.5 JPY/day** (43 JPY/day even at the claimed +0.30 bps). Validity (Q6): 330 ticker rows (0.097%) are crossed (ask ≤ bid, to −5 ticks); dropping them gives S1 +0.074 bps, t=0.76, 46.8% forced — nothing depends on them. Regimes (Q4): the effect is hold-time, not hour — hold terciles +1.39 / +0.70 / −1.12 bps (n=478/277/681), while all 24 hours lie in −0.99…+0.90 with n≈56–80 and no pattern.

## QA3-1

Denominator: 1,436 completed S1 round trips (entry fill → maker exit or 300 s forced exit) over 5.00 days,
2026-08-03…08-07; sd 3.689 bps. I get **+0.066 bps/round trip, t=0.68** — the claim's number is 4.5× mine and,
decisively, the claim's conclusion "positive and significant" does not hold: |t|=0.68, p≈0.50. The claimed
+0.30 bps is reachable only under the one reading that lets a *partial* fill open a position (+0.310,
t=3.89) — and that same reading puts the forced share at 33.9%, breaking QA3-6 (see Q9 below). No single
reading of the stated rule reproduces QA3-1 and QA3-6 together. MDE at n=1,436 is 0.273 bps (80% power, 5%,
two-sided), so an effect of the claimed size *would* have been detected; it is absent, not merely unresolved.
The claim also fails its own control test: a random-entry placebo on the same tape earns +0.978 bps.
Verdict: 結論変更

## QA3-2

Denominator: 17,841 round trips generated by replaying S1 with "first print at my price/side fills me
unconditionally". Mean **+0.843 bps** — the claimed +0.84 reproduces to 0.4%; t=93.38 vs 74.63 is 25% off
(the claim's t implies n≈11,500 against my 17,841, i.e. a different position count, not a different mean).
Note 0% of these exits are forced: with queue-ahead deleted, every exit fills before the cap, which is the
whole source of the optimism. The claim's *conclusion* is the problem and it is self-refuting: it concedes
the naive number is "大幅に楽観的" and then infers a tradable edge from it. An upper bound cannot establish a
lower one; under the rule the packet itself calls correct the same strategy yields +0.066 bps, t=0.68. The
gap +0.843 → +0.066 is 92% of the apparent edge, and it is exactly the fraction of orders that never reach
the front of the queue.
Verdict: 結論変更

## QA3-3

Denominator: completed S2 round trips (improve 1 tick inside when spread ≥ 2 ticks — 62.7% of ticker rows
have spread ≥ 2 ticks — else quote the touch; 300 s cap). Under price-through matching, n=3,124,
**−0.665 bps, t=−10.49** (validity-filtered: −0.666, t=−10.55); the sign is negative in all 16 readings
(−0.10 … −0.82) and significant in 6 of 8 S2 readings. The claimed magnitude −0.98 is 47% larger than my
best-matched reading and no reading comes within 10%; the claimed t=−26.19 implies n≈8,750, ~2.8× my count.
The conclusion (S2 is negative — improving inside the touch destroys the edge) survives, and my decomposition
explains it: improved quotes fill with queue-ahead 0, so they fill precisely when the book is about to move
through them — forced exits on S2 average −2.09 bps vs S1's −1.12. Under the strictly literal exact-price
reading the same population is only −0.181 bps, t=−1.05, i.e. the *significance* of this claim is an artefact
of which fill-matching convention is used; that ambiguity is a defect of the rule text, listed below.
Verdict: 数値差異(結論維持)

## QA3-4

Denominator: 1,436 S1 entry fills; adverse selection = sign-adjusted mid change from the entry-fill timestamp
to +5.0 s (mid from the last ticker row at or before each timestamp; long → +Δmid, short → −Δmid).
Result: **−0.460 bps in our favour-adjusted sign, t=−21.45**, i.e. the mid moves **against** the fill by
0.46 bps within 5 s, overwhelmingly significantly. MDE at this n is 0.060 bps, so "indistinguishable from
zero" would require |effect| < 0.06 bps; the measured effect is 7.7× the MDE. This is not a marginal call and
it is not reading-dependent — it is the mechanical consequence of a queue-position fill: a resting bid is
filled by the sell flow that is about to push the mid down. It is also the quantity that reconciles every
other claim (adverse selection 0.46 bps ≈ the gap between the 2.33 bps spread and the +1.13 bps actually
captured on non-forced exits). The claim states the opposite of what the data shows.
Verdict: 結論変更

## QA3-5

Denominator: 757 of the 1,436 S1 round trips whose exit filled as a maker before the 300 s cap (52.7%).
Mean **+1.134 bps, t=10.83** — 10.0% below the claimed +1.26, exactly on the band edge (through-reading:
+1.100, −12.7%; validity-filtered: +1.145). So the number is essentially reproduced. The conclusion is not:
this subset is defined *by its own outcome*. A position lands in it precisely when the market came back to
our exit price, which is the definition of a winner in this design; the 679 excluded trades average
−1.124 bps, and the mixture 0.473×(−1.124) + 0.527×(+1.134) = +0.066 bps is the actual expectation. Reporting
the non-forced subset as "the strategy's correct expected value" discards 47.3% of the population by outcome
— a survivorship estimate, off by a factor of 17. The random-entry placebo makes the same point: it too shows
+1.87 bps on its non-forced subset while being, by construction, signal-free.
Verdict: 結論変更

## QA3-6

Denominator: the same 1,436 completed S1 round trips; 679 hit the 300 s cap and were crossed out at the
displayed touch = **47.3%** (validity-filtered 46.8%, n=1,445; through-matching 42.9%, n=1,545). Against the
claimed 44.6% that is +6.1% relative, inside the 10% band under every reading I would defend as primary, and
the conclusion (roughly 45% of round trips end as takers at the cap) holds. Two caveats that do not change
the verdict: the share is not a property of the strategy but of the free cap parameter — 87.0% at 60 s,
75.8% at 120 s, 46.8% at 300 s, 21.2% at 600 s, 10.3% at 900 s — and my random-entry placebo also produces
44.6%, so this share carries no information about the strategy's edge (Q8: it is a first-passage property of
the price process at a 300 s horizon, not evidence of anything the strategy does).
Verdict: 再現

## Q7 / Q9 / Q10 (cross-claim)

Selection contamination: the cap is a free parameter and sweeping it alone moves the S1 net from −0.403 bps
(t=−8.95, 60 s) through +0.074 (300 s) to +0.531 (t=4.01, 900 s). A 5-point sweep on one parameter therefore
spans ±0.5 bps with |t| up to 9 on a population whose true mean is indistinguishable from 0; the claimed
+0.30 / t=2.79 is well inside the range a search of that size returns under the null, and the packet's other
free parameters (clip 0.05, improve threshold 2 ticks) are untested here. Arithmetic consistency: the claim
set {QA3-1, QA3-5, QA3-6} is self-consistent (0.446 × −0.893 + 0.554 × 1.26 = +0.30 bps) and so implies a
forced-exit mean of −0.893 bps; my measured forced-exit mean is −1.124 bps, and no reading of the rule
produces the triple (44.6%, +1.26, +0.30) simultaneously — the reading that gives +0.30 gives 33.9% forced,
and the reading that gives ~45% forced gives +0.07. **QA3-1 is the member that must be wrong**: QA3-5 and
QA3-6 are stable across readings while QA3-1's headline swings by a factor of 300 and its sign is not stable.
Falsification: "S1 as specified earns a positive net of ≥ +0.27 bps per round trip on this tape" — falsified
at n=1,436 (MDE 0.273 bps at 80%/5%; observed +0.066 ± 0.097 s.e.). "5 s adverse selection is |·| < 0.06 bps"
— falsified (observed 0.460, s.e. 0.021).

## 前提の誤り

- fill matching "executions at its price" | packet 約定規則 + manifest | exact-price matching makes an
  *improved* quote unfillable except by coincidence, so S2 fills 423 times vs S1's 1,436 while the manifest
  simultaneously asserts improved orders have queue-ahead 0; through-matching gives S2 3,124 fills | decides
  whether QA3-3 is significant (t=−1.05 vs −10.49); biases S2 toward "no effect" under the literal reading |
  inherited by every claim measured on a quote-improving or inside-the-touch maker strategy.
- "positions = completed entry fills" vs "or partially per FIFO" | 約定規則 | the two phrases contradict each
  other; treating a partial as a position changes n from 1,436 to 1,867 and the S1 net from +0.066 to
  +0.310 bps | this single ambiguity is the entire difference between QA3-1's claim and my result |
  inherited by every maker-fill claim in this packet, and by any live maker sizing that assumes all-or-none.
- ticker sizes "ALWAYS include our own resting clip ... for whichever maker strategy this packet's ticker
  reflects (S1)" | manifest | the tape is S1-conditioned, so S2 is replayed against a book that never
  contained S2's quotes and whose touch never reacts to S2 improving it | S2's numbers are not a like-for-like
  comparison with S1's; direction unknown but the S1-vs-S2 *difference* is contaminated | inherited by every
  cross-strategy comparison drawn from this one tape.
- own-size subtraction in queue-ahead | 約定規則 | ±0.05 on the subtraction moves the forced share from 47.3%
  to 54.8% and the net from +0.066 to −0.001 bps | biases QA3-1 upward as written | any claim quoting a
  queue-position fill rate.
- "net" | all six claims | fees are 0 bps in this packet (manifest; consistent with
  `config/products.yaml FX_BTC_JPY taker_fee_pct: 0.0`), so "net" = gross and the 47.3% of round trips that
  cross the spread as takers pay only the spread | no bias on this synthetic packet, but any transfer of
  +0.30 bps to a venue with a non-zero taker fee is invalid: 47.3% × any taker fee ≥ 0.7 bps erases it |
  inherited by every claim that reports a maker strategy's "net" from this generator.
- non-forced subset as the estimator | QA3-5 | conditioning on the exit having filled is conditioning on the
  outcome; excluded trades average −1.124 bps | biases the expectation upward by +1.07 bps (17×) |
  inherited by any claim that reports per-trade edge after dropping timed-out or forced-closed positions.
- forced-exit share as evidence | QA3-6 | 44.6% is reproduced by a signal-free random-entry placebo and moves
  from 87% to 10% with the cap alone | the share is a first-passage statistic, not strategy evidence |
  inherited by any claim citing a timeout rate as a property of a strategy.
- data validity | manifest (silent) | 330 crossed ticker rows (ask ≤ bid, to −5 ticks) and 1,030 duplicate
  timestamps are present and undocumented | negligible: removing them changes S1 by +0.008 bps | none.
