## Denominator (Q1)
16 trading days, 2026-08-20→2026-09-04. Ticker (best bid/ask/size) rows: 1,589,458; execution prints: 750,191.
Tick size = 1 JPY (derived from unique price diffs). Fee: `taker_fee_pct: 0.0` for `FX_BTC_JPY`
(`config/products.yaml`) — confirms claim's 0% fee. Taker exit cost modelled at 1bps per task spec.
Grid: offset{0,+1tick}×TP{5,10,20bps}×cap{30,120s} = 12 cells × fill-model{naive,queue,requote} = **36 cells**,
both buy- and sell-side quoting. Total simulated round-trip cycles: **2,388,690**. 19:00–19:15 UTC and crossed-book
rows excluded pre-simulation.

Own methodology (simplifications, disclosed): (a) one entry stream per (side,offset,fillmodel) generated by
continuous immediate re-quoting, then TP/cap exit applied per cell — re-entry after an exit is snapped to the next
already-computed fill candidate ≥ exit time (not a fresh re-simulation from that exact instant); (b) maker TP exit
triggers when best_bid (buy) / best_ask (sell) reaches the TP price within the cap window; otherwise forced taker
exit at cap (bid/ask − 1bps), **never dropped**, matching the spec; (c) "queue" fill = cumulative opposite-side print
volume at-or-through the quote price must exceed size displayed ahead at quote time (0 if the quote improves the
book by 1 tick); "requote" resets this each time the touch price moves.

## Headline result
| metric | value |
|---|---|
| cells exceeding +0.38bps net | **0 / 36** |
| best cell (point est.) | offset+1, fillmodel=queue, TP20bps/cap120s: **-1.60bps** [day-clustered 95% CI -3.54, +0.33], n=4,785 cycles/16d (~299/day), maker-close(TP-hit) rate 14.6% |
| best high-n (requote) cell | offset+1, TP20/cap30: **-2.14bps** [-2.24, -2.03], n=274,681 (~17,168 cycles/day), maker-close rate ~1% |
| pooled all 36 cells | -1.60 to -3.43bps, mean ≈ -2.3bps |

Naive/queue (non-requoting) models produce far fewer cycles/day (~290-300) than "fast-cycle" implies, because a
static resting order gets stranded once price trends intraday (observed: >17h with zero fills on trending days) —
this is itself evidence against the "fast cycle" premise for those two fill models. Only `requote` sustains
~17,000 cycles/day, and it is economically the worst-behaved (heaviest churn into the ~1bps taker floor).

## Controls (Q2)
- **Sign-reversed (buy vs sell)**: buy -2.16bps (n=1,164,174), sell -2.58bps (n=1,224,516) — both negative, similar
  magnitude, no directional artifact.
- **All-time random control** (enter at mid, no maker/queue logic at all, same TP/cap taker-fallback rule, 300
  random draws/day): **-2.06bps** [day-clustered se 0.096], n=28,800 — statistically indistinguishable in
  magnitude from the maker-fill cells.
- **State-conditional (vol tercile)**: low -2.03, mid -2.35, high -2.63bps — monotonically worse with vol, no
  regime where it flips positive.
- **By hour**: -1.90 to -3.02bps across all 24 UTC hours — none positive.

## Simplest alternative explanation (Q8)
The random-time, no-maker-logic control reproduces essentially the same loss magnitude (-2.06bps) as the real
maker-fill cells (-1.6 to -3.4bps). **This passes**: the loss is overwhelmingly explained by the TP/cap exit
structure itself (TP rarely reached within 30-120s; most cycles resolve as a taker exit paying the ~1bps
cost/spread-cross) rather than by adverse selection specific to being picked off as a passive maker. Bid-ask
bounce / repeated cost-crossing, not "getting run over," is the dominant mechanism.

## Capture/adverse decomposition (quote-time-mid referenced, per spec)
capture = (quote-time mid − fill price)/quote-time mid; adverse = (fill-time mid − quote-time mid)/quote-time mid
(signed so + = favorable to the position), both vs the **same** quote-time-mid reference as instructed.
naive: capture +1.27, adverse -0.09 (sum ≈ +1.18bps). queue: capture +1.29, adverse -0.27 (sum ≈ +1.02bps).
requote: capture +0.19, adverse +0.77 (sum ≈ +0.95bps). **All three are positive at the moment of fill** — the
edge is real and captured on entry — but it is fully consumed (and reversed) during the subsequent TP/cap hold:
full round-trip net is -1.6 to -3.4bps. This differs in **sign** from the claim's quoted
"capture+adverse(5s) = -0.716", see 前提の誤り below; the full round-trip net (the number actually compared to
the +0.38bps bar) is what carries the verdict, and it fails by 2-9x the bar's magnitude.

## Selection contamination (Q7)
Day-resampling bootstrap (500 draws, resample 16 days w/ replacement, recompute all 36 cell means, take the max
each draw): distribution of the best-of-36 cell mean has p5/p50/p95 = **-2.20 / -1.60 / -0.03**bps. Even under
full resampling uncertainty on the actual best cell, the 95th percentile stays below the +0.38bps bar — cherry-
picking among 36 cells could not plausibly have produced a pass.

## Consistency (Q9)
Independent reconstruction `board_round_series_5s.csv.gz` (249,336 5-second bars, same calendar span, built from
raw board snapshots rather than the ticker/executions tape): analogous random-mid TP10bps/cap120s test →
**-3.43bps** (n=41,551). Same sign, comparable-to-larger magnitude — consistent with the primary result.

## Data validity (Q6)
1,589,458 ticker rows, 1 duplicate timestamp, 6,902 gaps >5s (max 569s, reconnect-scale), 19:00-19:15 UTC and
crossed-book rows excluded. Negligible effect at this n; not re-run without the filter given budget.

## MDE / falsification (Q10)
Best (point-estimate) cell: 16 days, day-sd 3.95bps, se 0.988bps → 95% two-sided MDE ≈ **2.74bps**. The gap
between the best cell's point estimate and the +0.38 bar is only ≈2.0 SE (z≈2.0, p≈0.04 one-sided) for that one
low-n (queue, ~300 cycles/day) cell alone — a modest true edge near the bar could be missed by this design at
16 days. However the high-throughput `requote` cells (n>270k, se≈0.06bps) reject the bar by >20 SE — decisive
regardless of the low-n cell's imprecision. **Falsification**: a design of this size cannot rule out a true edge
of +0.3 to +1bps at the lowest-throughput cells, but can and does rule out the fast-cycle-scalper family as a
whole once the requote (highest-cycle) cells are included.
