1. **Q1 ranking by directness to the purpose.**
   - (a) Direct. It is gated minus ungated for form A, the quantity the purpose names.
   - (b) Direct. Same measure for form B. Mechanically it equals minus the calm-day profit divided by all classified days.
   - (e) Direct for the mechanism. It tests whether calm days lose money after the calm-day classification, the stated origin of the gate.
   - (f) Direct as a contrast. It shows whether the middle and volatile days differ from calm days, which the gate assumes.
   - (h) Proxy. Year-by-year sign checks stability of (a) and (b), not the effect itself.
   - (c) Proxy. Profit per traded day ignores the excluded days, so it cannot show gain over ungated.
   - (d) Proxy. Same flaw as (c), and it covers a different number of days than (c).
   - (g) Proxy. It tests sensitivity to the fill assumption, not the gate.
   - (i) Unrelated as a measurement. It is bookkeeping, useful only to read the other numbers.

2. **Q2 covariates.**
   - Fill assumption: report. Both forms share one run and one fill, so it is only varied through (g).
   - Year: report. Needed for (h), and the calendar mix differs between the classes.
   - First vs second half: report. The source finding was stated per half, so the halves must be shown.
   - Market: report. It is constant within the run, but it bundles currency, spot vs margin and venue, so no single cause can be named.
   - Number of traded days: report. A trades about a third of days and B about two thirds, so noise differs.
   - Positions carried across the session boundary: match. Count them and apply one rule to both forms, otherwise gating is confounded with carry.
   - **Fairness of the all-days average:** it is fair for the paired same-day difference, which gives total gain over ungated.
   - It is not fair for comparing A with B, because zeros dilute them by different shares.
   - Show the per-traded-day profit and the share of days alongside, and do not rank A against B on the diluted figure alone.

3. **Q3 not measured.** Transaction costs are set to zero, though the strategy flips position every minute and calm days have small moves. Realistic costs would likely make calm days even worse and could make the ungated strategy negative overall, so the conclusion could change.
