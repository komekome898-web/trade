# Q1: Observable ranking by directness

**Direct:**
- (a) A minus ungated, per day, averaged: Measures exactly whether gate A changes profit vs baseline strategy.
- (b) B minus ungated, per day, averaged: Measures exactly whether gate B changes profit vs baseline strategy.

**Proxy:**
- (c) A's profit per traded day: Shows A's absolute profitability but lacks ungated comparison baseline.
- (d) B's profit per traded day: Shows B's absolute profitability but lacks ungated comparison baseline.
- (e) Ungated profit on calm-day-after, by half: Shows ungated exposure to filtered days; suggests gate impact indirectly.
- (f) Ungated profit on middle/volatile day-after, by half: Shows ungated performance on other terciles; contextual.
- (g) Sign of (a) and (b) under midpoint fill: Tests robustness to fill assumption, secondary to core effect.
- (h) Year-by-year sign of (a) and (b): Stratifies gate effect; useful but less direct than overall effect.

**Unrelated:**
- (i) Number of traded days per form: Observation count only; does not measure gate effectiveness.

# Q2: Covariate handling

| Covariate | Treatment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Fill assumption | MATCH | Both gated and ungated must use same fill (next open) to isolate gate mechanism. |
| Year | REPORT | Gate uses year-based tercile cutoff; robustness across years validates generalization to Binance. |
| First vs second half | REPORT | BitFlyer gate worked asymmetrically by period; Binance must show whether this pattern holds. |
| Market (Binance vs bitFlyer) | IGNORE | Different markets tested separately; not a within-Binance covariate. |
| Number of traded days | IGNORE | Consequence of gating, not an independent adjustment variable. |
| Overnight positions | MATCH | Verify strategy flattens as designed; ensure no session-boundary violations confound results. |

**Averaging fairness:** YES, fair. Setting excluded days to zero profit is what the gated strategy literally does. Comparison correctly measures gate's impact on total profit (avoiding losses or missing gains on filtered days).

# Q3: Critical unmeasured factor

**The gate's predictive mechanism**: The design measures whether previous-day volatility *improves profit when used as a filter*, but not whether Binance's previous-day volatility *predicts today's mean reversion strength* the way it does on bitFlyer. If the gate succeeds because it filters universally bad days (illiquidity, wide spreads) rather than low-reversion days, the conclusion that "the mechanism is the same" would be misleading.
